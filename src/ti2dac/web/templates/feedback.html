{{ ui.icon("refresh") }}How feedback works

One loop per mistake: the rule gets fixed now, and future rules avoid the same mistake.

  1. 1
    ReportOn a rule's Feedback tab: what is wrong (artifact, category, severity) and why. Saved against that rule version.
  2. 2
    FixPaste a corrected rule (validated before it is accepted){% if ai_enabled %}, or let the AI rewrite it from your comment{% endif %}. The fix becomes the next version; the old one is kept in history.
  3. 3
    Re-reviewThe new version starts as a draft, queries are re-converted and test results reset. Someone other than its author approves it.
  4. 4
    LearnThe comment becomes a lesson, redacted and added to future generation prompts for the same ATT&CK technique or log source.

Feedback on behaviours, simulations or the ADS is recorded for evaluation (export below) but does not change prompts unless it is turned into a lesson.
